# Before Redemption

Read this before redeeming your key. These account and hardware requirements apply across the WardenGG Launcher (both World of Warcraft and Overwatch 2).

## TPM 2.0 Requirement

> **TPM 2.0 is required for all WardenGG products**
>
> Check that your PC supports TPM 2.0 before redeeming your key. WardenGG cannot run without it.
>
> Not sure how to check? Follow [Enable TPM 2.0](enable-tpm.md) for Windows checks and BIOS instructions by motherboard brand.

## Windows Requirements

- **Windows 10 Pro (22H2)** or **Windows 11 Pro (22H2, 24H2, or 25H2)**. Windows Home and other editions are not supported.
- **TPM 2.0** enabled in BIOS/UEFI, **Secure Boot** disabled, **HVCI / Core Isolation** disabled, **Vulnerable Driver Blocklist** disabled, and third-party antivirus uninstalled.

See [Getting Started](getting-started.md#preparations) for the step-by-step commands.

---

## What do I do after redeeming my key?

1. Run `SystemChecker.exe` from Discord to verify your PC configuration.
2. Complete [Getting Started](getting-started.md) to prepare Windows and download the WardenGG Launcher.
3. Follow your game's setup guide:
   - **World of Warcraft:** set up your script folder and `.wgg`/`.lua` files in [Scripts & Management](wow-scripts.md).
   - **Overwatch 2:** no script files needed; follow [Overwatch 2 First Steps](overwatch-setup.md) for video settings and mouse calibration.
